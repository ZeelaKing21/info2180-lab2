My First Night in Survival Mode

October 3, 2026 by Jahchrisi Beadle

I know every first time players remember their first night in Survival mode. I spawned in a small village and spent the whole day punching trees, collecting wood and crafting some tools. By the time the sun sets, I had a wooden pickaxe, a sword and enough cobblestone to build a shelter for the night.

When night fell, the zombies and skeletons came out and I heard all the creeper hissing somewhere outside my wall. I stayed inside, crafted a furnace and cooked some food until morning. The next day I finally found iron, made a proper set of tools and to started build a real house near the village. Note to self: always build your shelter before the night falls!

Exploring the Forest Biome

October 5, 2026 by Jahchrisi Beadle

This week I left my base to explore a huge forest biome to the east. The forest was fill with oak and birch trees, flowers and plenty of animals, which made it the best place to collect wood and food for the trip. I marked my path using torches so I would be able to find my way back.

Deep down in the forest, I found the entrance to a cave and went in. It was pitch black and full of mobs, but it was worth it because I found and collected coal, iron and even a few diamonds!On my next adventure, I'm going to use the diamonds to craft a diamond pickaxe and start building my own mine.
